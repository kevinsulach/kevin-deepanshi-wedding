TO USE YOUR OWN MUSIC:

1. Drop your song here, e.g.  public/music/wedding.mp3
   (mp3 format works best on both Android + iPhone)

2. Open src/config.ts and change:

     musicUrl: null

   to:

     musicUrl: "./music/wedding.mp3"

3. Push to GitHub — the site rebuilds automatically.

Leave musicUrl as null to keep the built-in soft instrumental
(no file needed, plays instantly, no copyright issues).

You can delete this .txt file — it is only instructions.
